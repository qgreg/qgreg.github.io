# Sports in Space — Visual Asset Generation Prompts

Standalone image-generation prompts for the concept art slots in
[`sports-in-space.html`](sports-in-space.html). Each is written to be pasted
directly into ChatGPT / DALL·E with no additional context.

Save each result as `<asset_tag>.jpg` in `theme/images/sports-in-space/` and the
page picks it up automatically.

## House style

All prompts share one visual standard, restated inline in each so they stay
standalone:

- **Style & tone** — hard sci-fi architectural realism, grounded industrial
  engineering, realistic structural scale, cinematic stadium lighting, believable
  athletic gear. No cartooning, no fantasy tropes, no neon vaporwave.
- **Composition** — wide-angle or volumetric interior views showing clear athletic
  mechanics, structural enclosure (dome trusses, hull rings, canyon cables), and an
  unmistakable visual indicator of the gravity regime: midair hang time, curved
  Coriolis ball paths, or anchored footing.
