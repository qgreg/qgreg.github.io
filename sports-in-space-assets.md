# Sports in Space — Visual Asset Prompts

One concept-art slot per sport, keyed to the `asset_tag` in
[`sports-in-space.json`](sports-in-space.json). Each prompt is standalone —
paste it straight into ChatGPT / DALL·E with no extra context.

Save the result as `<asset_tag>.jpg` in `theme/images/sports-in-space/`;
the page fills each slot automatically and falls back to a labelled
placeholder until then.

**House style (restated inline in every prompt so they stay standalone):**
hard sci-fi architectural realism, grounded industrial engineering, realistic
structural scale, cinematic stadium lighting, believable athletic gear. No
cartooning, no fantasy tropes, no neon. Compositions are wide-angle interiors
that show the *enclosure* — the roof, its span and its height — because
enclosure volume is what this page is about.

## `basketball_planetary_dome`

```text
Interior of a low-gravity planetary basketball arena under a vast pressurized dome, hard science fiction realism. Regulation court far below towering eight-metre elevated rim assemblies mounted on engineered steel columns; several athletes suspended in mid-air at the top of impossibly long jumps, limbs extended, clearly hanging rather than falling. High dome trusses and pressure glazing overhead with a dim planetary landscape beyond. Cinematic arena lighting, realistic athletic gear with visible micro-spike footwear, photorealistic wide-angle architectural rendering, no cartooning, no neon.
```
