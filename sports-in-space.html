Sports in Space

The Big Five team sports were tuned, over a century, to one gravitational constant and one atmosphere. Move them off Earth and the rules break before the athletes do. This is a reference matrix for where they break, and what each sport becomes on the other side — across planetary gravity wells and engineered orbital habitats.

Visual asset pipeline

Until then the slot shows the tag and its generation prompt. Prompts are written standalone for ChatGPT / DALL·E and share one house style: hard sci-fi architectural realism, real structural scale, cinematic stadium lighting, no cartooning or neon.

Method & caveats

  • The matrix is a design reference, not a simulation. Gravity, apex and clearance figures are physical; constraints and adaptations are engineering judgement about what a sport becomes once those figures bind.
  • Apex multipliers are 1/g relative to Earth, which is exact for vacuum ballistics. The soccer row deliberately breaks from that — at 0.15 g under 1.5 atm, aerodynamic stall cuts the ideal 6.7× back toward 6×, and drag, not gravity, is writing the rules.
  • Jumping and throwing scale cleanly with gravity. Running does not. Sprinting depends on the normal force gravity supplies; below roughly half a g athletes bounce instead of stride and get slower, not faster. That is why the constraint column keeps returning to traction rather than speed.
  • Zero-g rows have no apex at all — flight is linear, so the ballistics half of the calculator does not apply to them. Their limit is Newton's third law: every action a player takes moves the player.
  • Bodies are the unmodelled variable. Months at low gravity thin bone and shrink cardiac muscle, so off-world leagues would likely be contested by residents, not visiting Earth athletes — and the two could not safely share a field.
